Write a detailed essay of approximately 8000 words titled "The History of Computing: From
Mechanical Calculators to Machine Intelligence". Organize it into at least twelve numbered sections,
each with a descriptive heading. Cover, in order: early mechanical computing and Babbage/Lovelace;
the mathematical foundations (Boole, Hilbert, Godel, Turing); vacuum-tube machines and the first
electronic computers; the stored-program idea and early commercial systems; the transistor and
solid-state electronics; integrated circuits and Moore's law; mainframes and time-sharing; the
microprocessor and personal computing; software, operating systems and programming languages; computer
networks and the Internet; mobile and embedded computing; and the rise of machine learning and
artificial intelligence.

Requirements:
- Be precise with dates, names, and technical details. Give concrete examples.
- Maintain a consistent, formal, expository tone throughout. No bullet lists; flowing prose only.
- Each section must be substantial (several hundred words). Do not rush.
- Do not stop early, do not summarize prematurely, and do not repeat yourself. Write the full essay
  through the final section and then a short concluding section.
